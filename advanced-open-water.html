Getting your Advanced Open Water 🦈

The next step after Open Water. Five fun "adventure dives", certification to 30m, and the key to the region's bucket-list sites.

What is it? 🤔

Advanced Open Water (AOW) is less scary than it sounds. There are no exams and no pool sessions, just five guided "adventure dives" where you try different kinds of diving with an instructor.

Two dives are compulsory: a deep dive and a navigation dive. You choose the other three. Pass, and you're certified to dive to 30m.

What you need before signing up ✅

  • Open Water certification (PADI, SSI or equivalent). You can do AOW right after OW, even on the same trip.
  • Age 12 or older. Divers aged 12–14 are limited to 21m.
  • A few hours of eLearning (about 6–8 hours, covering the dives you've chosen).
  • Updated medical form if anything has changed since your OW.
💡 Many divers do Open Water and Advanced back to back on one trip, about 5 days total, to save on flights.

How the course works 📚

  1. Pick your 3 electivesChoose with your instructor based on where you're diving (ideas below).
  2. eLearningShort theory for each adventure dive, done before you fly.
  3. Deep dive (compulsory)Below 18m, down to about 30m. You learn to handle depth, gas planning and narcosis.
  4. Navigation dive (compulsory)Compass and natural navigation, so you can lead your buddy back to the boat.
  5. 3 elective dives → certified 🎉Usually 2 days in total, with 2–3 dives a day.

Popular electives near Singapore 🌊

  • Peak Performance Buoyancy: the most useful one; you'll float better and use less air.
  • Night: sleeping parrotfish, hunting octopus, glowing plankton.
  • Wreck: great in Tulamben (Liberty wreck) or on Bintan and Batam liveaboards.
  • Enriched Air Nitrox: longer bottom time. Some agencies count it as an elective.
  • Drift: perfect for Nusa Penida, Komodo or Similan currents.
  • Fish ID or Underwater Naturalist: fun in Anilao or Moalboal.
  • Digital Underwater Photography: learn to shoot those mantas properly.

What it unlocks 🔓

30mmax depth (21m if aged 12–14)
🧭you can navigate your own dives
♾️never expires

Dives you can now do

  • Sipadan: AOW is required to get a permit.
  • Malapascua threshers: shops require AOW for the dawn dive.
  • Mola mola at Crystal Bay (Bali): they usually sit at 25–30m.
  • Richelieu Rock & Koh Tachai (Similan): AOW recommended for the currents.
  • Komodo & Raja Ampat liveaboards: most boats expect AOW.
  • Tubbataha: AOW plus about 50 logged dives.

Good to know

  • Being certified to 30m doesn't mean every dive should go there. Plan conservatively, especially after a break.
  • Nitrox is the next most useful add-on for liveaboards (more bottom time, less tiredness).
  • After AOW, many people do Rescue Diver. It's tough but rewarding, and makes you a much better buddy.
