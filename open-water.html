Getting your Open Water licence 🐠

Your first scuba certification: what it is, how to get it from Singapore, and what it lets you do.

What is it? 🤔

Open Water Diver is the first full scuba certification. It's the "licence" dive shops ask to see before they'll take you diving, rent you gear or fill your tank.

The two big agencies are PADI and SSI. Their Open Water courses are equivalent and accepted worldwide, so pick the dive school you like rather than the agency.

What you need before signing up ✅

  • Age 10 or older. Under 18s need a parent or guardian to sign the forms.
  • Basic swimming: tread water or float for 10 minutes, and swim 200m (or 300m with mask, fins and snorkel). No time limit, and you don't need to be fast.
  • Medical questionnaire: you'll fill one in. Some conditions, medications or recent surgery need a doctor's sign-off first, so check early.
  • Time to do the online theory (eLearning, about 5–10 hours) before your pool sessions.
💡 You don't need your own gear. Courses include rental, though many people buy their own mask early for a better fit.

How the course works 📚

  1. Theory (eLearning)Online lessons and quizzes at your own pace, about 5–10 hours. Do it at home before the trip so your holiday is all diving.
  2. Confined water (pool) sessionsLearn the skills in shallow water: clearing your mask, recovering your regulator, buoyancy, sharing air.
  3. 4 open water divesRepeat the skills in the sea (or a big habitat), usually no deeper than about 12m during training.
  4. Get certified 🎉Your digital card shows up in the PADI or SSI app. Screenshot it for dive shops.
✈️ Don't fly for about 18 hours after your last dive. Book your flight home for the day after.

Where to do it if you're in Singapore 📍

Most Singaporeans do theory and pool sessions at home, then the open water dives somewhere clearer. Here are the popular routes:

🐋

Singapore Oceanarium (Sentosa)

No leave · Fri–Sun course
S$678 all-in
  • Run by Resorts World Sentosa. Your first open water dive is inside the giant Open Ocean Habitat with thousands of fish.
  • Includes eLearning, certification and gear.
  • No refunds after registering, and a minimum of 4 people for weekday classes.

After you're certified 🎉

18mmax depth (12m for ages 10–11)
👯always dive with a buddy
♾️never expires
  • You can now book fun dives and trips worldwide, rent gear and join most dive boats.
  • Stay within 18m and your training. Some famous dives are deeper and need Advanced: Sipadan, the Malapascua threshers, and the mola mola at Crystal Bay.
  • Haven't dived in 6–12 months? Do a quick refresher (PADI ReActivate or SSI Scuba Skills Update). Many shops require it.
  • Log your dives. Some trips, like Tubbataha, need a minimum number of logged dives.
  • Easy next steps: Advanced Open Water (to 30m), Enriched Air Nitrox (longer bottom time), or Peak Performance Buoyancy.
🎀 Great first trips after certifying: Tioman, Tulamben (Bali), Moalboal, Anilao and Koh Tao are all OW-friendly. Use the "OW-friendly" filter on the home page.
